## Ways to get involved

There are many ways to strengthen your neighborhood and the city as a whole.

- **Join** — add your voice as a member. It's the fastest way to stay informed and be counted.
- **Volunteer** — help with outreach, research, events, or your neighborhood association.
- **Attend** — come to public meetings and hearings; watch the Calendar for what's next.
- **Share** — tell your neighbors and share our work on social media.

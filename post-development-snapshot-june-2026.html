What’s rising across West Palm Beach: a development snapshot

June 17, 2026 · WPBRC

« All posts

Our development tracker now follows dozens of major projects across West Palm Beach — completed, under construction, approved, proposed, and on hold. Here’s a brief snapshot of the landscape.

The waterfront keeps building up

North and South Flagler Drive continue to see a wave of residential towers. These projects bring investment and new residents, but also raise real questions about traffic, density, and public access to the water — exactly the citywide issues the Coalition follows.

Downtown and the corridors

Downtown, CityPlace, and the Dixie Highway corridor (including SoSo) are adding offices, hotels, and mixed-use space, while Northwood and the North End see large redevelopments. Use the map to filter by status and district to see what’s near you.

This is a working summary; verify specifics against current City of West Palm Beach agendas. See the full, filterable map on the Development page.

Welcome to the WPBRC Blog

June 18, 2026 · WPBRC

« All posts

Welcome to the West Palm Beach Residents Coalition blog. This is where we’ll share clear, non-partisan updates on the issues that affect our whole city — new development projects, upcoming public meetings and hearings, and the long-term planning decisions that shape our neighborhoods.

What to expect

Each week we’ll summarize what’s moving on the development tracker, flag the meetings worth showing up for, and explain what it means for residents — in plain language, with sources.

Stay in the loop

Prefer it in your inbox? Sign up for our newsletter, and consider joining the Coalition to add your voice.

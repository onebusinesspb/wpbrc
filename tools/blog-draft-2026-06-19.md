# What's up for review this summer: seven projects to watch

**Date:** June 19, 2026 · **Author:** WPBRC · **Tag:** Update

*Seven major projects are currently under city review — the stage where resident input matters most. Here's where they are concentrated and what to keep an eye on.*

---

The clearest moment for residents to weigh in on a new building is while it is still under review, before approvals are final. Our development tracker currently lists seven projects in that window, and they cluster in two parts of the city.

## Northwood and North Flagler

Most of the activity is along the North Flagler waterfront and into Northwood. Proposals in review there include 2001 N. Flagler Drive (a 27-story, 90-unit condominium from Related Group and BH Group), the 30-story Alba Reserve, Jeff Greene's two-tower Currie Towers, and the eight-story, 140-unit Deco Northwood. The remaining phases of the NORA District — adding residential, hotel, retail, and public space — are also under review.

## SoSo and South Dixie

Two projects are moving in the SoSo / South Dixie corridor. Jeff Greene's South Dixie housing development would rise about 25 stories with 366–399 apartments, 40% of them designated workforce housing, and is notable for a planned pre-fabricated timber structure now before the Plans and Plats Review Committee. Nearby, Tricera Capital's redevelopment of the former Palm Beach Post site — The Press — would bring roughly 125,000 square feet of retail anchored by an organic grocer, plus office space.

## And one to watch downtown

Separately, Vanderbilt University's planned graduate campus downtown is advancing. The university announced it has secured initial funding and authorized construction to begin on the roughly $520 million campus, which is expected to focus on business, data science, artificial intelligence, and engineering.

## Why it matters

Projects in review still have public meetings ahead — Planning Board hearings, City Commission readings, and Downtown Master Plan workshops — where residents can ask questions about traffic, density, height, and waterfront access. Check the events page for meetings worth showing up for, and use the filterable map on the development page to see what is near you.

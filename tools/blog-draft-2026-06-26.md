# Tracker update: one tower finishes, three more take shape

**Date:** June 26, 2026 · **Author:** WPBRC · **Tag:** Development

*Alba Palm Beach has finished on North Flagler, while new proposals at The Press, the NORA District, and Good Samaritan signal where the next round of building may land.*

---

This week's scan of city sources and local development press points to one project crossing the finish line and several new ones moving onto the horizon. Here is what changed and what it means for residents. The full, filterable map lives on our development tracker.

## Finished: Alba Palm Beach

The 22-story, 55-residence Alba Palm Beach tower at 4714 North Flagler Drive in Northwood has reached completion, with move-ins now underway. It is one of the first newly finished waterfront residential buildings along this stretch of North Flagler, where investment north of downtown continues to grow. We are moving this project to *Completed* on the tracker.

## On the horizon: three proposals to watch

Three projects are being reported but have not yet reached a formal city vote, so we are watching them rather than treating them as approved. At the former Palm Beach Post site — *The Press* — a 350-unit residential building with parking and ground-floor restaurant space has gone through an early pre-application review, alongside the previously reported retail and office plans. In the NORA District, *Nora House* would bring 117 for-sale condominiums and roughly 18,900 square feet of ground-floor retail, with a groundbreaking targeted for 2027. And near Good Samaritan Medical Center, a proposal pairs two roughly 28-story condominium towers with hospital, housing, and care facilities; no formal application has been filed yet.

## Why it matters

Early-stage proposals are exactly when resident questions about traffic, density, height, and waterfront access carry the most weight — before plans are locked in. As these move forward, they will appear on Planning Board, City Commission, and Downtown Master Plan agendas.

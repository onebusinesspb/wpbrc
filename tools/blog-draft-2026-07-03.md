# Two new tower proposals: downtown and North Flagler

**Date:** July 3, 2026 · **Author:** WPBRC · **Tag:** Development

*This week's scan turned up two newly proposed condominium projects — a downtown tower on Hibiscus Street and a two-tower plan across from Currie Park on North Flagler.*

---

Two new condominium proposals surfaced in local development press this week, both still early in the city's review process. We are adding them to our watch list rather than treating them as approved, and sharing what is known so far. The full, filterable map lives on our development tracker.

## Downtown: 400 Hibiscus Street

A proposed tower at 400 Hibiscus Street, near CityPlace, would bring 88 residences along with ground-floor retail and resident amenities. It is being developed by Mast Capital with partners and has been publicly branded as Banyan Tree Residences West Palm Beach. Reported heights have varied between coverage, and the building's final height is one detail worth confirming as it moves through City Commission review. The project has already cleared early city committee steps.

## North Flagler: 2085 N. Flagler Drive

Near Northwood and across from Currie Park — which is undergoing a roughly $35 million renovation — Terra and BH Group have proposed two 31-story towers with 281 condominiums, ground-floor retail, and parking. The 1.5-acre site is reported to be under contract for about $100 million. This proposal has not yet reached a formal city vote.

## Also this week

Alba Palm Beach, the 22-story waterfront tower at 4714 N. Flagler Drive that we noted last week, is now confirmed complete with move-ins underway. We are updating its status on the tracker from Approved to Completed.

## Why it matters

Early-stage proposals are exactly when resident questions about height, density, traffic, and waterfront access carry the most weight — before plans are locked in. As these projects advance, they will appear on Planning Board, City Commission, and Downtown Master Plan agendas. Check the events page for meetings worth attending, and use the tracker to see what is near you.

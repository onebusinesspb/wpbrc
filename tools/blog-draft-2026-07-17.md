# Waterfront building pause goes to a final vote Monday, July 20

**Date:** July 17, 2026 · **Author:** WPBRC · **Tag:** Update

*The City Commission takes its final vote July 20 on a six-month moratorium on new high-rise applications along the Flagler waterfront corridor. Here's what the pause would — and wouldn't — do.*

---

One week after pausing the Downtown Master Plan update, the city is poised to take a second, more concrete step. On Monday, July 20 at 5:00 PM, the City Commission holds its final (second-reading) vote on a proposed six-month moratorium on new planned development applications along the Flagler waterfront corridor. Commissioners approved the ordinance on first reading last week.

## What the moratorium would do

The pause applies to properties south of Monroe Drive, north of Southern Boulevard, west of Flagler Drive, and east of Washington Road — the stretch where developers have pursued condo buyouts of older buildings and proposed a wave of luxury towers. If adopted, the moratorium runs through January 20, 2027, unless the Commission extends it. During the pause, the city plans to hire planning firm Zyscovich to complete a zoning and economic analysis and draft new zoning regulations for the corridor.

Commissioner Stephen Sylvester, who represents the South End, called the proposal a direct response to residents' concerns about overdevelopment, and said he hopes it is the start of a broader plan that doesn't rely on granting an ever-increasing number of zoning variances.

## What it would not do

The moratorium pauses *new applications* only. Projects already approved or under construction — and applications already filed — are not affected. You can see where each tracked project stands on our [development tracker](development.html).

## Also this week

One West Palm, the two-tower Quadrille Boulevard project that broke ground in 2019, is reported to be nearing completion — the developer faces a $5 million penalty if it isn't finished by the end of 2026. And the proposed 18-story convention-center hotel at 900 S. Rosemary, approved by the Planning Board in June, still awaits a City Commission hearing date; we're watching the agendas.

## How to weigh in

Public comment is taken at Commission meetings. Monday's meeting is at 5:00 PM in Commission Chambers, City Hall, 401 Clematis Street. The Planning Board also meets Tuesday, July 21 at 6:00 PM. Details and future dates are on our [events page](events.html); confirm agendas on the city's website before attending.

# A quieter week — and a packed August at City Hall

**Date:** July 31, 2026 · **Author:** WPBRC · **Tag:** Update

*After July's South Flagler vote, this week brought no major city decisions — but August's meeting calendar is full, the FY27 budget survey is open, and four tracker updates are queued.*

---

After a July dominated by the South Flagler zoning pause, this was a quieter week at City Hall — no major development votes. But August is shaping up busy, and several projects moved in ways worth catching up on. As always, the full picture lives on our [development tracker](development.html) and [events page](events.html).

## August at City Hall

The City Commission meets three times in August — the 3rd, 17th, and 31st, all at 5:00 PM in Commission Chambers at 401 Clematis Street — with the CRA Board meeting at 2:00 PM ahead of the August 3 session. The Downtown Action Committee, which reviews appeals and special-use permits in the Downtown Master Plan area, is expected August 12 at 9:00 AM, and the Planning Board hears development applications August 18 at 6:00 PM. The Historic Preservation Board normally meets the fourth Tuesday, August 25. Confirm agendas on the City's website before attending.

One more date: the City's FY27 Proposed Budget Community Survey is open now and closes September 3 at 5:00 PM. It's a direct way to weigh in on spending priorities before budget hearings in September.

## Catching up on the tracker

Four updates are queued for review. The 27-story, 90-unit tower at 2001 N. Flagler Drive — now branded Rosewood Residences by Related Group and BH Group — won city approval in June. At 120 S. Dixie Highway, Jeff Greene's 25-story tower is now proceeding under the state's Live Local Act as a mixed-income project incorporating the former fire station. Related Ross closed on a $55 million, 2.2-acre South Dixie site for a fourth condo project of roughly 100–130 units. And in Northwood, a seven-story affordable housing project is proposed at 611 27th Street.

Also of note: the 400-unit Ponte Verde condominiums at 1401 Village Boulevard sold for $90 million to Kamson Corporation — a large residential sale we'll watch for any redevelopment signals.

# The boom's ripple effects — and new movement on the tracker

**Date:** August 7, 2026 · **Author:** WPBRC · **Tag:** Development

*West Palm Beach's growth is now a regional conversation, with Palm Beach leaders raising traffic concerns. Meanwhile, affordable housing funding, a major land sale, and a $180M construction permit moved this week's tracker.*

---

West Palm Beach's building boom is no longer just a West Palm Beach conversation. This week, leaders across the Intracoastal in Palm Beach raised concerns at their Town Council meeting about the pace of growth on our side of the water — with traffic topping the list. Officials there reviewed more than 100 downtown projects that are planned, approved, under construction, or completed, spanning thousands of residential units and over 2 million square feet of commercial space. Whatever your view of the boom, its effects clearly reach beyond city limits — and regional coordination on traffic and infrastructure is worth watching. Our [development tracker](development.html) maps the full picture.

## New on the tracker's radar

Three items moved recently that residents may have missed. The City approved $640,000 in federal HOME funding on July 20 for Roseland Gardens Phase II, a plan to rebuild aging public housing into 100 affordable rental homes. The City is also advancing a $6.6 million sale of 57 acres to Pine Crest School for a private school campus. And downtown, the long-stalled West Palm Point office tower secured a $180 million construction permit in July — a sign that project is moving in earnest.

## The convention center hotel, and fall ahead

Related's 400-room hotel at 900 S. Rosemary Avenue cleared the Planning Board in June, and the developer has said it aims to break ground around the end of August — though City Commission and county approvals are still pending, with no hearing dates set. Separately, the City has said it will relaunch community engagement on the Downtown Master Plan this fall after pausing committee reviews.

## Dates to know

The City Commission meets August 17 and August 31 at 5:00 PM, and the Planning Board hears applications August 18 at 6:00 PM — all in Commission Chambers at 401 Clematis Street. And the FY27 Proposed Budget Community Survey closes September 3 at 5:00 PM. Full listings on our [events page](events.html).

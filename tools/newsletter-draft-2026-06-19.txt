SUBJECT: Seven WPB projects are under review right now — here's where to weigh in

WEST PALM BEACH RESIDENTS COALITION
Weekly Update · June 19, 2026

Hello neighbors,

The best time to ask questions about a new building is while it's still under review — before approvals are final. Seven major projects are in that window right now. Here's a quick look, plus the meetings worth tracking.

DEVELOPMENT WATCH

- North Flagler & Northwood lead the pipeline. Five proposals are under review there, including 2001 N. Flagler Drive (27 stories, 90 units), the 30-story Alba Reserve, Jeff Greene's two-tower Currie Towers, and the remaining phases of the NORA District.

- SoSo / South Dixie is busy too. Jeff Greene's South Dixie housing development (about 25 stories, 366–399 apartments, 40% workforce housing) is before the Plans and Plats Review Committee, and Tricera Capital's redevelopment of the former Palm Beach Post site — The Press — would add ~125,000 sq ft of retail with an organic grocer.

- Vanderbilt's downtown campus is advancing. The university has secured initial funding and authorized construction to begin on its roughly $520M graduate campus focused on business, data science, AI, and engineering.

- See it all on the map. Our tracker now follows 50 projects citywide — completed, under construction, approved, in review, and on hold. Filter by status and district on the development page: https://wpbrc.org/development.html

UPCOMING MEETINGS

No new dated public hearings are confirmed for the next few weeks as of this writing. Keep an eye on these recurring forums where residents can speak:

- City meetings to watch: Downtown Master Plan workshops, Planning Board hearings, City Commission readings, and CRA Board meetings.
- Coalition Board Meeting: held at least quarterly; members welcome. We'll share the next date here in the newsletter.

Always confirm dates and times against the current City of West Palm Beach agenda before attending.

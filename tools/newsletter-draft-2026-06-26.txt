SUBJECT: One waterfront tower wraps up — and three new proposals to watch

West Palm Beach Residents Coalition — Development & Meetings Update

Hi neighbor,

Here is your short read on what is moving across West Palm Beach — one project finished, three new ones worth watching, and how to stay involved. As always, this is non-partisan and fact-based: issues, not personalities.

DEVELOPMENT WATCH

- Alba Palm Beach is finished. The 22-story, 55-residence waterfront tower at 4714 N. Flagler Drive in Northwood has reached completion, with move-ins underway — one of the first newly completed buildings on this stretch of the corridor.

- The Press (former Palm Beach Post site). A 350-unit residential building with ground-floor restaurant space has gone through early pre-application review, alongside previously reported retail and office plans. Not yet voted on.

- Nora House, NORA District. A proposed 117-unit, for-sale condominium with ground-floor retail; groundbreaking targeted for 2027.

- Good Samaritan area. A proposal pairs two roughly 28-story condominium towers with hospital, housing, and care facilities. No formal application filed yet — we are watching.

UPCOMING MEETINGS

No fixed-date city hearings are confirmed on our calendar for the next few weeks, but these are always worth tracking:

- City meetings to watch (ongoing): Downtown Master Plan workshops, Planning Board hearings, City Commission readings, and CRA Board meetings.
- Coalition Board Meeting: quarterly — date and location announced here in the newsletter.

Confirm dates and agendas on the events page (https://wpbresidents.org/events.html) and the City of West Palm Beach agenda portal before attending.

GET INVOLVED

Resident questions about traffic, density, height, and waterfront access matter most while projects are still in review. Three ways to help:

- Join the Coalition: https://wpbresidents.org/join.html
- Donate: https://wpbresidents.org/donate.html
- Forward this email to a neighbor.

— The WPBRC team

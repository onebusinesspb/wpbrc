SUBJECT: Two new tower proposals — and one waterfront project now finished

West Palm Beach Residents Coalition — Development & Meetings Update

Hi neighbor,

Here is your short, non-partisan read on what is moving across West Palm Beach this week — two newly proposed towers to watch, one project now confirmed complete, and how to stay involved. As always: issues, not personalities.

DEVELOPMENT WATCH

- 400 Hibiscus Street (downtown). A proposed condominium tower near CityPlace would bring 88 residences with ground-floor retail and amenities. Developed by Mast Capital with partners and publicly branded as Banyan Tree Residences West Palm Beach. It has cleared early city committee steps; reported heights vary, so final height is worth confirming as it advances.

- 2085 N. Flagler Drive (Northwood / North Flagler). Terra and BH Group have proposed two 31-story towers with 281 condominiums, retail, and parking, on a site across from Currie Park (now undergoing a roughly $35M renovation). Not yet voted on.

- Alba Palm Beach is finished. The 22-story waterfront tower at 4714 N. Flagler Drive is now confirmed complete with move-ins underway. We are updating its status to Completed on the tracker.

See the full, filterable map on the development page: https://wpbresidents.org/development.html

UPCOMING MEETINGS

No fixed-date city hearings are confirmed on our calendar for the next few weeks, but these are always worth tracking:

- City meetings to watch (ongoing): Downtown Master Plan workshops, Planning Board hearings, City Commission readings, and CRA Board meetings.
- Coalition Board Meeting: quarterly — date and location announced here in the newsletter.

Confirm dates and agendas on the events page (https://wpbresidents.org/events.html) and the City of West Palm Beach agenda portal before attending.

GET INVOLVED

Resident questions about height, density, traffic, and waterfront access matter most while projects are still in review. Three ways to help:

- Join the Coalition: https://wpbresidents.org/join.html
- Donate: https://wpbresidents.org/donate.html
- Forward this email to a neighbor.

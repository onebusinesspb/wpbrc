SUBJECT: Downtown Master Plan votes postponed to fall — resident input made the difference

West Palm Beach Residents Coalition — Development & Meetings Update

Hi neighbor,

Big news this week: the city has paused the Downtown Master Plan update. After sustained input from residents and neighborhood organizations — including this Coalition — Mayor Keith James announced on July 7 that the planned votes are postponed until fall, with a commitment to a more transparent process. Here's your short, non-partisan read on the week.

DEVELOPMENT WATCH

- Downtown Master Plan paused. The proposed update would allow towers up to 25 stories along Flagler Drive (above a 5-story baseline), reduce parking requirements, and add larger waterfront setbacks. A committee review set for early July and a tentative August 31 Commission vote are now postponed to fall. The city says residents will get direct access to staff and technical experts before decisions are made. We'll share new hearing dates as soon as they're published.

- The Berkeley Palm Beach broke ground July 6. The 25-story condominium at 550 S. Australian Avenue, overlooking Clear Lake, is now under construction — see it on our tracker.

- Nora House (Northwood / NORA District). Sales are underway for an 11-story, roughly 117-residence condo — the NORA District's first for-sale building. Groundbreaking is anticipated in 2027; still early, and we're watching it.

- Downtown Development Authority appointment. On July 6, the City Commission voted 4-1 to appoint a Related Ross executive to the DDA board. Some residents raised questions given the firm's extensive downtown holdings; we'll keep following DDA business on the issues.

See the full, filterable map on the development page: https://wpbresidents.org/development.html

UPCOMING MEETINGS

The Master Plan hearings that had been expected this summer are postponed to fall — no new dates yet. In the meantime, these are always worth tracking:

- City meetings to watch (ongoing): Planning Board hearings, City Commission readings, and CRA Board meetings.
- Coalition Board Meeting: quarterly — date and location announced here in the newsletter.

Confirm dates and agendas on the events page (https://wpbresidents.org/events.html) and the City of West Palm Beach agenda portal before attending.

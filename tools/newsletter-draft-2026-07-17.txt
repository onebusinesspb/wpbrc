SUBJECT: Final vote Monday: a six-month pause on new waterfront high-rise applications

West Palm Beach Residents Coalition — Development & Meetings Update

Hi neighbor,

Last week the city paused the Downtown Master Plan update. This Monday, July 20, the City Commission takes a final vote on something more concrete: a six-month moratorium on new high-rise applications along the Flagler waterfront corridor. Public comment is taken at the meeting. Here's your short, non-partisan read on the week.

DEVELOPMENT WATCH

- Waterfront moratorium — final vote Monday, July 20 at 5 PM. The ordinance would pause new planned development applications for properties south of Monroe Drive, north of Southern Boulevard, west of Flagler Drive, and east of Washington Road, through January 20, 2027 (unless extended). Commissioners approved it on first reading last week. During the pause, the city plans to hire planning firm Zyscovich to study the corridor's zoning and draft new regulations. Note: already-approved and under-construction projects are not affected.

- One West Palm nearing completion. The two-tower, 30-story Quadrille Boulevard project (offices, 326 residences, 201-key hotel) that broke ground in 2019 is reportedly in its final stretch — the developer faces a $5 million penalty if it isn't done by the end of 2026.

- 900 S Rosemary convention-center hotel — still awaiting a Commission date. The Planning Board approved the 18-story, 400-room hotel in June; final City Commission approval is pending and no hearing date has been set. We're watching the agendas.

- Rosewood Residences (2001 N. Flagler) approved. The city greenlit the 27-story, ~90-unit luxury condo by Related Group and BH Group in June. It's moving to Approved on our tracker.

See the full, filterable map on the development page: https://wpbresidents.org/development.html
